PART I

Item 1. Business
Microsoft Corporation is a global technology leader operating at the forefront of the generative artificial intelligence era. In fiscal year 2024, Microsoft completed the strategic acquisition of Activision Blizzard, expanding our gaming and interactive entertainment business. Our Azure cloud platform achieved massive expansion, powering enterprise AI workloads globally. We expanded Copilot integration into Windows, Office 365, Security, and Azure AI Studio. Microsoft operates global datacenter regions across more than 60 regions worldwide, continuously scaling capital investments in high-density AI accelerators and liquid cooling infrastructure.

Item 1A. Risk Factors
Risks affecting Microsoft's business operations and strategic outlook:
• NEW DISCLOSURE: High dependence on advanced AI hardware, specialized semiconductor accelerators (GPUs), and electrical power generation. Inability to secure adequate high-performance computing clusters and power grid access could constrain Azure AI scaling and inflate operating costs.
• NEW REGULATORY SCRUTINY: Heightened global antitrust examination of partnerships and equity investments with generative AI frontier labs, including regulatory inquiries from the FTC, CMA, and European Commission.
• Cybersecurity threats targeting cloud authentication tokens, source code repositories, and identity platforms have escalated in sophistication from nation-state actors.
• Rapid evolution of generative AI capabilities may cannibalize traditional per-seat licensing models if enterprises transition to autonomous agent architectures.
• Integration challenges, goodwill amortization, and regulatory commitments associated with the Activision Blizzard acquisition.

PART II

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations (MD&A)
In fiscal year 2024, total revenue was $245,122 million, an increase of 16% compared to $211,915 million in fiscal year 2023. Revenue growth was led by Intelligent Cloud and Microsoft Cloud, which reached $137 billion, up 23%.
Cost of revenue was $74,015 million, yielding gross profit of $171,107 million and an expanded gross margin of 69.8%.
Operating income grew 24% to $109,433 million, representing an operating margin expansion to 44.6% from 41.8% in fiscal year 2023.
Net income rose 22% to $88,136 million, with diluted earnings per share of $11.80 per share.
Reflecting our commitment to leading the AI platform transition, capital expenditures surged to $44,477 million, compared to $28,107 million in the prior year, focused on datacenter footprint, GPU clusters, and high-speed networking.
Cash generated by operating activities was $118,548 million, supporting free cash flow of $74,071 million. Total debt was $56,750 million, and cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments stood at $75,536 million.
